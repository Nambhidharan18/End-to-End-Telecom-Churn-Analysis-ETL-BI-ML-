# 🔎 Business Problem & Key Findings

### 1. What are the major reasons customers stop using telecom services?

The analysis indicates that **customer commitment, service experience, and competitive pressure** are important factors behind churn. Customers on month-to-month contracts are particularly vulnerable because they have fewer barriers to switching providers. The high churn contribution from fiber optic customers also suggests that pricing, service reliability, or customer expectations may need further attention.

### 2. Which customer segments or service attributes contribute the most to churn?

Churn is strongly associated with **contract type, internet service, geography, and customer demographics**. Month-to-month customers show the highest churn probability, while fiber optic users contribute significantly to overall churn. Certain geographical regions also show substantially higher churn, indicating that local market conditions or service quality may influence customer decisions.

### 3. How can the company reduce churn and improve long-term customer retention?

The company should focus on **proactive and targeted retention strategies** rather than treating all customers equally. High-risk customers can be identified early and offered personalized plans, loyalty benefits, service improvements, or incentives to move toward longer-term contracts. Regional churn patterns and fiber service concerns should also be investigated and addressed.

### 4. What factors are the strongest predictors of future churn?

The analysis suggests that **contract type is one of the strongest predictors of churn**, particularly for month-to-month customers. Other important factors include **service type, geography, and customer characteristics**. The machine learning model also demonstrates the value of identifying high-risk customers early, allowing the company to intervene before they leave.
